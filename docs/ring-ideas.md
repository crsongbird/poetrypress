# The ring's idea book

Ruby's notes on the Ringworld, each expanded into what it could become, with more beside them (Ruby, 2026-10-24:
"Expand on the ideas I gave you and add more, interesting things to it"). The frame for all of it is Ruby's: "remaking
Elder Scrolls Arena basically and setting it on a ringworld (also with transporters to other places like mars and
other lands)". So every idea here is something a painting can show today and a game could walk into later. Every idea
here is approved for production (Ruby, 2026-10-25). What is built leaves this page for the changelog; what is chosen to build goes to OPEN-ISSUES.md or ROADMAP.md. The research
behind it: docs/prior-art/ringworld-wildlife.md, docs/prior-art/arena-on-a-ringworld.md, and the ringworld docs beside
them.

## 1 · Life that moves

- **Startled by passers-by.** A flock on the grass lifts when a walker, a tractor or a droid comes within a few metres,
  and settles again behind it; a herd lifts its heads at a cart on the road. Every mover is already a pure function of
  the clock, so "is anyone near this flock at this moment" can be asked without remembering anything.
- **Who follows whom.** Egrets stalking at the feet of grazing cattle; crows mobbing a hawk across the sky; gulls behind
  the plough (there now) and behind the fishing boats as they come in; swallows over the cattle for the flies they
  raise; a dog walking with its person, running ahead and back.
- **The day's rhythm.** Rooks streaming home to a rookery at dusk in a long loose river across the sky; starlings'
  murmuration over the roost (there now); an owl leaving a barn at nightfall and quartering the field; the dawn chorus
  as small birds lifting off the hedges at first light; deer coming out of the wood at dusk to the field's edge.
- **Weather in the animals.** Cattle lying down before rain; swallows low before a storm (there now); birds sitting out
  a downpour hunched on the fences; deer sheltering under the wood's edge in snow, their backs white.
- **The towns' own.** Cats on walls and sills, pigeons on the cathedral's ledges, a heron on a canal's bollard, koi in
  the neon city's canals, a stray dog asleep in a doorway's light, gulls on every roof by the harbour.
- **Ruins reclaimed.** Feral horses on the old roads, crows on the toppled statues, ivy and birds' nests in a fallen
  giant's open chest, foxes denning under a wreck.
- **Builders' herds.** Maintenance drones flocking like starlings; a field of dishes turning together like grazing
  heads (there now: they track one target, each a moment late); service crawlers that scatter from a walker like crabs.

## 2 · The sky and what is in it

- **Planets with weather.** Banded giants whose storms turn and drift; a ring's shadow across its planet; moons in
  transit throwing tiny shadows on the giant; a planet's night side pricked with city lights; aurora rings at its poles.
- **Moons of every kind.** A cratered grey one, an ice one with geysers lit by the star (Enceladus), a volcanic one with
  a glowing night side (Io), a shattered one trailing its own debris ring, a double planet, a moon close enough to see
  its mountains on the terminator.
- **Other stars.** A red dwarf's salmon light and long shadows; a blue-white star's hard cold day; binary suns casting
  double shadows that cross at noon; a white dwarf's tiny fierce point; a pulsar's slow lighthouse sweep (on the gentle
  clock); a star in a nebula, its sky full of glowing gas.
- **Galaxies.** An accurate Milky Way band and the Magellanic Clouds for Sol's rings; Andromeda as a pale smear; from a
  galaxy's halo the whole spiral standing over the arch; a globular cluster's sky, a thousand bright stars and no dark;
  a ring round a black hole with its light bent into an arc.
- **The air's optics.** A rainbow opposite the sun after rain; sun dogs and a 22° halo in thin cirrus; crepuscular rays
  under a broken cloud deck; sun pillars at a cold dusk; noctilucent clouds after sunset; the glory round your own
  shadow on fog below a hill.
- **Rayleigh everywhere.** The planets' limbs blue and their terminators reddened; the ring's far arch going rose at its
  own dusk; the star's colour carried into every light on the land.

## 3 · Weather that tells its story

- **Storms built as storms.** An anvil's top spreading at the tropopause, a shelf cloud's dark lip leading the gust
  front, rain curtains hanging in several veils that fade in and out (Ruby's notes), the land darkening under it and
  bright again behind.
- **Wind you can read.** Grass waves running across a field, cat's-paws on a lake, trees leaning and showing their
  pale undersides, smoke laid flat, washing on lines, flags, the kite's string (there now), dust off a dry track.
- **The morning.** Mist pooled in the valleys at dawn and burning off as the sun climbs; frost on the shaded side of
  every roof; dew greying the grass until the sun reaches it.
- **Seasons.** By the seed or across a long film: blossom, high summer, the woods turning, bare branches and snow.
- **Dry country's own.** Heat shimmer over the far road, a haboob's wall of dust coming across the plain, dust devils
  (there now).

## 4 · Places to walk into (the Arena frame)

- **Towns that are places.** Walls and gates, an inn with its sign, a temple, a guild hall, a market on market day with
  its stalls, banners on the castle; every town named by the seed and its name on the milestones of the roads to it.
- **Ways in.** A dungeon's mouth in a hillside, a crypt's stair in the ruins, a tower's door, a well that goes down too
  far; each a place a later game opens, a mystery a painting only shows.
- **Transporters.** A ring of standing stones or of glass pylons with a shimmering gate, and through it another world:
  Mars's rust plain, a candy world's pink hills, an Enceladan ice field under Saturn. The See Through machinery that
  bends one surface through another could paint the far world inside the gate.
- **The ring's own geography.** Cities climbing the rim wall, elevators running up it, the spill mountains at the wall's
  foot, a pilgrim road along the ring with its waystations, a river crossing the whole band, the shadow squares' night
  sweeping across the land.
- **The order to build it in**, each step improving the paintings too: docs/prior-art/arena-on-a-ringworld.md §5.3
  (where on the ring, regions as arcs, names, the world's ways, building roles, people by role, the ground camera,
  interiors, dungeons, quests, the map, the gates).
- **Travellers.** Caravans on the long roads, a knight riding, pilgrims with staves, a merchant's cart, a mail coach;
  each with somewhere to go and a timetable to get there.

## 5 · Folk of the wilds and the strange

- **Elves and fae in place of people.** Tall slender figures with lanterns walking the wild trails at dusk; small
  winged lights dancing in the fairy rings at night; a fae market of glowing stalls that is there only by moonlight.
- **Gem walkers and other constructs.** Faceted golems walking the ley lines, light caught and split in their bodies,
  glowing from inside at night; brass automatons tending a wizard's garden; a stone giant asleep as a hill whose chest
  rises once an hour (a time-lapse shows it breathe).
- **Slow giants.** A treant that walks a metre an hour, only a time-lapse sees it move; a sky whale drifting along the
  arch; a dragon circling a peak on the thermals as a buzzard does (the raptor's flight, made huge).
- **Demonic and eldritch.** Obsidian spires veined with red light; a black sun; rain falling upward in one valley;
  statues that always face you however the camera turns; a door standing alone in a field that opens at night; a herd
  that moves in perfect unison, which is the wrong thing; something very tall walking in the fog at the edge of sight.

## 6 · Other worlds, as very rare rings

- **Mars.** Rust dunes and dust devils, the blue sunset that Mars really has, Phobos and Deimos racing, pressure domes
  and rovers, terraced canyon gardens under glass, frost on the rocks at dawn.
- **Venus.** Cloud cities floating in the golden haze, airships between them, the sun a bright smudge, sulphur-yellow
  rain that never reaches the ground.
- **Enceladus.** White plains striped with blue tiger-stripe rifts, geysers lit against a black sky with Saturn huge,
  ice caves glowing blue, the ocean's light coming up through the cracks; once in a long while, on an ice ridge, a
  seven-tailed fox of light: the moon's guardian.
- **Candy.** Gumdrop hills, candy-cane trees, a chocolate river, cotton-candy clouds, sprinkles for snow, liquorice
  roads, gingerbread towns, gummy deer grazing; all drawn by the same generators with candy's materials.

## 7 · The film studio

- Kinesis (its tab, two cameras, the sun's arc, keys on a reel): docs/prior-art/film-studio.md.
- Beyond it: a film that follows a creature (the camera keyed to a deer's own path, which is a pure function of the

  time-lapse whose animals come and go with their hours.

## 1 · People

- An adult figure is "approximately seven to eight heads" tall; a seven-year-old about six heads, a fourteen-year-old
  about seven; a newborn about four [src](https://www.drawingforall.net/human-body-proportions/).
- The female pelvis is relatively wider; in a typical male figure the shoulders are noticeably wider than the pelvis,
  in a typical female figure the difference smaller; these "vary considerably from person to person"
  [src](https://www.drawingforall.net/human-body-proportions/).
- Skin: the Monk Skin Tone Scale is "a 10-shade scale" made with Dr. Ellis Monk to be "more inclusive of the spectrum
  of skin tones", which people with darker skin found represented them better than the industry's standard
  [src](https://blog.google/technology/ai/monk-skin-tone-scale/).

  pointed ears, long hair), dwarves (short, broad, bearded) and fae (small, winged, lit); among the builders, androids.

## 2 · A deer's day

- Red deer spend from 20% to 90% of their time feeding by season; rest takes about 50% to 60% of the time in winter and
  40% to 60% in summer, and deer chew the cud lying down; they alternate feeding and ruminating in cycles of 5 to 9
  hours; a sleep bout is "30 seconds to a few minutes of dozing" with alert spells between, deer standing to stretch
  about once a half-hour cycle [src](https://www.wildlifeonline.me.uk/animals/article/red-deer-activity).
- So in the painter [est]: between its moves each deer of a herd keeps its own slow rhythm, grazing with its head down,
  standing with its head up to look round, or lying down to rest and chew, its head up, a member at a time; a herd at
  any moment some of each. Ground birds hop now and then, flicking their wings.

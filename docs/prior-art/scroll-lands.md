# Prior art: the lands of an ink scroll

Research for Sunrise Scroll's next pass. The artist loves the surface and asks for more variety by the seed and more depth: "the game had a wide variety of lands". Seven lands are researched here: misty peaks, a coast with curling waves, the snowy north, a bamboo forest, a village of roofs and gates, fields under a great tree, and a cursed land under a miasma. The surface's first round, and the methods it already uses (kouroku, mokkotsu, bokashi, the parchment filter), are in cel-shaded.md §7. The rule stays the same: original lands in the tradition, never a game's characters, creatures, logos or exact places. Public-domain works (Guo Xi, Sesshū, Hokusai, Hiroshige) are described here for their technique.

**Tags.** [src](URL) marks a claim from a source opened and read for this note. [est] marks an estimate or a design judgement.

## Summary

- Ink paintings build depth by tone, and the tone has names. Guo Xi's high distance is "clear and bright", deep distance "heavy and dark", and level distance mixes light and dark [src](https://mimesisjournals.com/ojs/index.php/aisthesis/article/download/4221/3320). Ink comes in five grades: charred, concentrated, heavy, light and clear [src](https://www.inkston.com/community/forums/topic/five-grades-ink). Five depth bands, one grade each, is a ready-made value ladder [est].
- Bare paper does the most work: it is snow [src](https://education.asianart.org/resources/whirling-snow-on-the-river-bank/), mist [src](https://actavictoriana.ca/culture/the-art-of-nothingness-an-approach-to-appreciating-chinese-art/), the river merging with the sky [src](https://tangpoetry.substack.com/p/wang-wei-views-with-a-painterly-eye), and the open ground that "makes a small palette look stronger" [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106559). Every land should decide what it leaves unpainted before deciding what it paints [est].
- Texture strokes (cun) belong to landforms. Axe-cut strokes suit hard, pyramidal rock and run at right angles to the contours. Hemp-fiber strokes suit gentle, earthy domes and cluster by the outer edges of curves. Bent-ribbon strokes suit slabs and terraces [src](https://noteaccess.com/Texts/Wong/WongT2.htm). A land kind can therefore choose its stroke [est].
- Scale lives in a tiny thing. "If a mountain is ten feet high, the trees should be one foot, the horse one inch, and a man the size of a bean" [src](https://berneval.hcommons.org/?p=5321). Guo Xi's tiny people act as guides through the picture [src](https://www.learner.org/series/art-through-time-a-global-view/the-natural-world/early-spring/).
- The brush-painted wolf game made its cursed land read as a "glowing red cursed zone", "bleak, dirty and bland" [src](https://gamesradar.com/okami-hands-on), under a "choking, otherworldly fog", with purple blight and dead, blackened trees. Restoration made the grass "explode with vibrant green foliage" and the trees burst into cherry blossom [src](https://pcgamer.com/why-i-love-restoring-nature-in-okami).
- Its world was a set of distinct land kinds: a farming village between mountains by the sea, wide grassland by a lake, a bamboo-covered pass, a hidden bamboo forest, a windy hilltop village, a fishing coast with cliffs and islands, a capital on a lake, a snowy northern island with a frozen lake and an evergreen maze [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/Nippon). We take the variety of kinds, never the places.

## 1. Misty peaks

**What makes it recognizable.** Peaks rise through mist that "bites into and seems to dissolve the mountains" [src](https://www.learner.org/series/art-through-time-a-global-view/the-natural-world/early-spring/). A serpentine "dragon vein" organizes the mountain [src](https://www.learner.org/series/art-through-time-a-global-view/the-natural-world/early-spring/). Forms are left partly visible and partly hidden (ma) [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sh%C5%8Drin-zu_by%C5%8Dbu).

**How painters build it.**
- The order is the outer contour, then the inner contours, then the texture strokes, then a tonal wash that "helps to pull the textures together". The wash makes light and dark "not related to any single light source" [src](https://noteaccess.com/Texts/Wong/WongT2.htm).
- Guo Xi layered ink washes to build forms [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guo_Xi). The softer areas of Early Spring hold seven to eight layers of ink, and his rock strokes are "cloud-resembling" [src](https://depts.washington.edu/chinaciv/painting/tptgnsla.htm).
- The named texture strokes are axe-cut, hemp-fiber, alum-head, raveled-rope, tangled hemp-fiber and raindrop, and the Mustard Seed Garden manual illustrates more than 30 [src](https://aisf.or.jp/~jaanus/deta/s/shunpou.htm).
- The axe-cut stroke is triangular, made with a slanted brush. Hemp-fiber is a sinuous line in layers. Bent-ribbon is a horizontal line bent sharply down [src](https://noteaccess.com/Texts/Wong/WongT2.htm).
- A sumi-e peak without outline (mokkotsu): the brush is held at 45°, light ink in its body and dark at the tip, pulled from the peak toward the lower right. The left side is then finished in lighter ink [src](https://japanobjects.com/features/sumie).
- Mi-style hills are made of "blots of wet ink", moist brushes and horizontal strokes, giving "a blurry, yet simplified mountain landscape" [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloudy_Mountains). Haboku lays dark ink rapidly over wet light washes, with no contour lines, and accents the result with a few dots of very dark ink [src](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/53228).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- the stroke family: axe-cut crags, hemp-fiber domes, or Mi-dot wet hills;
- the season, after Guo Xi's four: spring mountains "light and seductive as if smiling", summer blue-green, autumn "bright and tidy", winter "sad and tranquil as if sleeping" [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guo_Xi);
- whether one central peak stands over the rest (Fan Kuan's monumental type) [src](https://depts.washington.edu/chinaciv/painting/tptgnsla.htm);
- a waterfall or a temple placed high on the slope.

**Numbers.** Pyramidal mountains are drawn with lines falling 45° to the left and right [src](https://noteaccess.com/Texts/Wong/WongT2.htm). Early Spring is 83 × 43½ in [src](https://mofa.fsu.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2018/05/Early-Spring-by-Guo-Xi-Abby-Mann.pdf).

## 2. A coast with curling waves

**What makes it recognizable.**
- The wave "enters from the left and arches across most of the sheet", with the mountain a small triangle low in its trough [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106559).
- Its crest has "claw-like froth", and the spray falls "like snow" onto the peak [src](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_2008-3008-1). Others describe the leading edge as "numberless tentacles" [src](https://blogs.ubc.ca/geog481menglong/?p=8).
- A smaller wave repeats the peak's shape in miniature: "repeating a shape at two sizes ties foreground and background together" [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106559).
- Three boats head into the wave [src](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/A_2008-3008-1), each with about eight rowers [src](https://blogs.ubc.ca/geog481menglong/?p=8).

**How it is built.**
- In the print, the dark stripes in the wave's hollow were laid down first and then filled with pure Prussian blue. Printing one blue over another darkens the colour without dulling it [src](https://www.metmuseum.org/it/essays/hokusai-great-wave).
- The blue runs from deep in the belly to brighter shades [src](https://diluo.digital.conncoll.edu/Asianart/?p=2562). The foam is pale green-grey (about #C8D3C5) on cream paper (about #F9E9C6). The three blues cover only about a fifth of the sheet, and the sky is a darkened grey band [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106559).
- The design took decades. By 1803 the wave was "suggested by minimalist contours", and in 1805 it moved to the other side of the scene, the layout the final print keeps [src](https://mymodernmet.com/hokusai-great-wave-evolution).
- In Chinese ink, Ma Yuan painted waves with a "trembling brush" and crests "resembling curly tentacles" [src](https://theme.npm.edu.tw/Spotlight11206/en/page-3).
- Seigaiha is the calm, far sea as pattern: arcs of concentric circles overlapping so that "only the upper portion of each set" shows [src](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/seigaiha).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- the sea state: calm seigaiha, swell, or one great curling wave;
- which side the wave enters from;
- the coast: cliffs and islands, or sand and a fishing village [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/Nippon);
- a small boat for scale, as in level-distance paintings [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trees,_Level_Distance).

**Numbers.** The print is 25.7 × 37.8 cm, and the breaker is estimated at 9.7 to 11.8 m from the size of the boats. Its curl fits a golden spiral [src](https://www.surfertoday.com/surfing/the-great-wave-off-kanagawa-fascinating-facts-about-the-famous-japanese-print).

## 3. The snowy north

**What makes it recognizable.** The land is white because it is left unpainted, and the sky around it is darkened. In Night Snow at Kambara the sky is a dark grey fading toward the hills, and the roofs and road are bare paper [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106666). The snow seems to glow "with an inner radiance" [src](https://honolulumuseum.org/stories/2020/12/asian-art-2/hiroshiges-night-snow-at-kambara/).

**How painters build it.**
- The painter paints "the forms around the water or beneath the snow", and the blank areas read as snow. Graded washes of pale ink, light yellow and brown darken everything that isn't snow. White dots go over black accent dots, and the river is "hardly painted at all" [src](https://education.asianart.org/resources/whirling-snow-on-the-river-bank/).
- Sesshū's Winter Landscape uses short, straight lines of varying thickness and value, mostly at sharp diagonals, and middle greys "to build up the weight of winter". The only whites are around the tree branches. One heavy dark vertical line slashes down just left of centre [src](https://smarthistory.org/?p=43175).
- In woodblock, snow is blank paper, gauffrage (the block pressed in without pigment), flakes cut into the block, or gofun white splashed from a brush or sprayed [src](https://www.japaneseprints-london.com/6264/winter-landscapes). In Kambara the flakes are "spots where the carver cut the block away" [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106666).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- falling snow or a clear cold day;
- night (a sky dark to the horizon) or day (a pale grey sky);
- a frozen lake left as paper, or a dark open river;
- snowy evergreens, or a village of wood with snow-laden roofs [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/Nippon).

**Numbers.** Every landscape set by Hokusai and Hiroshige had at least one snow scene, and the One Hundred Views of Edo had up to seven [src](https://www.japaneseprints-london.com/6264/winter-landscapes).

## 4. A bamboo forest

**What makes it recognizable.** Straight, segmented stalks, and leaves "thin and translucent" at varied angles [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamboo_painting). In Driving Rain at Shōno, the thickets are layered silhouettes in three greys, printed from three blocks, to suggest distance [src](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/126086).

**How painters build it.**
- The order is stem, knot, branches, then leaves, the manual's "first principle" [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bamboo_painting).
- Segments are shorter near the top and the roots and longer in the middle. A joint is drawn like the character 心 without its dots [src](https://www.inkston.com/stories/mustard-seed-garden/4gentlemen-bamboo/), or with a horizontal-and-hook stroke [src](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/confucius/courses/brush-painting/course-outline.pdf).
- For the stalk, flatten the bristle tips and put dark ink at the sides, so the middle reads as a highlight [src](https://japanobjects.com/features/sumie).
- Each leaf is one stroke: press down in the centre, then lift slowly to a point [src](https://oreilly.com/library/view/sumi-e-painting/9781633228139/xhtml/ch06.xhtml). The leaf groups are the characters 个 and 人 [src](https://www.inkston.com/stories/mustard-seed-garden/4gentlemen-bamboo/).
- The groups are named for their shapes: fish tail for young, spring or overhanging bamboo; flying goose and swallow's tail for older bamboo [src](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/confucius/courses/brush-painting/course-outline.pdf).
- Paint the centre leaf of a group first, then the left and right. Leave a gap between leaf and branch. Paint the near leaves dark and the far ones light [src](https://japanobjects.com/features/sumie). Indigo can deepen a grove [src](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/confucius/courses/brush-painting/course-outline.pdf).
- Bamboo's strokes are thin, wide and side strokes; it is one of the four gentlemen [src](https://japaneseculturecenter.com/sumi-e-painting/).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- young bamboo (fish tail) or old bamboo (goose and swallow);
- calm, wind (the leaves streaming one way) or rain (Shōno's slanted grey streaks) [src](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/126086);
- a mountain pass with a path, or a closed grove [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/Nippon).

**Numbers.** Three greys for three depths [src](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/126086); two or three leaves to a group [est from the characters].

## 5. A village of roofs and gates

**What makes it recognizable.**
- In Shōno the village is "layered dark silhouettes of trees and house rooftops" [src](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/126086).
- Yamato-e cloud bands "obscure and divide space", and roofs are removed to show the rooms below (fukinuki yatai) [src](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/yamato-e-painting).
- Sesshū sets his temple in an open, lighter area, which isolates it [src](https://smarthistory.org/?p=43175). Distant houses "suggest the idea of habitation" [src](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/confucius/courses/brush-painting/course-outline.pdf).

**How the parts look.**
- **Tile roofs** are fired clay, "medium to dark grey": broad concave tiles, with half-round convex tiles laid over their seams [src](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/kawarabuki).
- **Thatch** is about 35 cm thick, and 90 cm or more at the eaves. The ridge is finished as a rounded roll or held by crossed strips [src](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/kayabuki). Steep gasshō roofs are pitched at 45° to 60° [src](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/node/4484).
- **Torii** come in two families. The shinmei is all straight parts: two pillars, a lintel (kasagi) and a tie beam (nuki) [src](https://silpasastradityam.substack.com/p/temple-gateways-nihon-architecture). The myōjin's lintels curve upward, the kasagi black and the shimaki beneath it vermilion [src](https://muza-chan.net/japan/index.php/blog/japanese-spiritual-architecture-myojin-torii).
- **Pagodas** have an odd number of storeys, three or five in wood. Their eave edges form a straight line, each one shorter than the one below [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_pagoda).
- **The generator's way.** {Shan, Shui}* draws a two-storey building as boxes with a roof, imitating a brush line by offsetting two noisy lines and filling the thin shape between them [src](https://zverok.space/advent2021/day24).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- a farming village or a hilltop village, a capital by a lake, or a temple on the mountain [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/Nippon);
- thatch or tile; a torii or not; a pagoda or not;
- whether cloud bands cut the village into scenes.

## 6. Fields under a great tree

**What makes it recognizable.**
- The sacred tree (shinboku) is "often distinctly visible due to the shimenawa wrapped around them". Cedars at Ise are among the named sacred trees [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinboku). Trees inhabited by spirits wear the rope, and cutting them is thought to bring misfortune [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimenawa).
- A rope strung across a space is thick in the centre and tapers to the ends. A rope tied round a sacred object keeps one thickness. Zigzag-folded washi streamers (shide) hang from it [src](https://www.nippon.com/en/views/b05204/shimenawa-the-sacred-rope.html).
- Terraced paddies look like "glistening steps climbing the side of a mountain" [src](https://amam.oberlin.edu/files/news/publications/amam-green-japan/8/). Sarashina's terraces were "an ideal spot for the moon's reflection", at a moon-viewing place known since the Heian period [src](https://risdmuseum.org/art-design/collection/moon-reflected-rice-fields-sarashina-shinano-province-shinshu-sarashina).

**How painters build it.** Paddies get "ink tones for reflective water", and terraces are painted "with dry ink" [src](https://hummedia.manchester.ac.uk/institutes/confucius/courses/caligraphy/chinese-brush-painting.pdf). Cherry hills read as "pink and white clouds of flowering cherry trees" [src](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/49001). Old bare trees take Guo Xi's "crab-claw" branches [src](https://depts.washington.edu/chinaciv/painting/tptgnsla.htm).

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- the tree: an evergreen camphor or cedar, or a cherry in bloom;
- the fields: terraced on a hill, or flat paddies;
- the paddies' stage: flooded mirrors, green, or gold;
- a single small shrine gate at the tree's foot.

**Numbers.** One shrine camphor is "about 24 metres around" and said to be over 2,000 years old [src](https://hiddenjapan-gems.com/kinomiya-shrine-atami/). A shrine's rope can be 1.8 m across; the largest is 13.5 m long [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimenawa).

## 7. A cursed land under a miasma

**What makes it recognizable.**
- In the brush-painted wolf game, a cursed zone shows on the map as black and red ink over an area. Within it, landmarks, buildings and nature disappear. Its sequel gave cursed zones purple skies [src](https://okami.fandom.com/wiki/File:Curse3.jpg).
- The land is "bleak, dirty and bland" under a "glowing red cursed zone" [src](https://gamesradar.com/okami-hands-on). There is fog, purple blight, "sickly, purple-tinged grass", dead blackened trees and withered clover [src](https://pcgamer.com/why-i-love-restoring-nature-in-okami).

**How restoration is shown.** Painted grass "explode[s] with vibrant green foliage", dead trees burst into cherry blossom, and a running trail leaves blooming plants behind it [src](https://pcgamer.com/why-i-love-restoring-nature-in-okami). The land "suddenly explodes with trees, cherry blossoms, and animals" [src](https://www.pcgamer.com/au/4k-gallery-the-beautiful-painted-landscapes-of-okami), and it opens into a "magnificent, blue-skied field" [src](https://gamesradar.com/okami-hands-on).

**How to paint it in the tradition** [est]:
- the miasma as a bokashi rising from the ground, not falling from the sky: Sun Hue red at the land's foot, fading upward;
- black haboku blots bled into it [src](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/53228);
- the land's wash pulled toward grey-violet;
- bare crab-claw trees;
- ash flakes instead of petals.

**What a seed could vary** [est]:
- red, black or violet miasma;
- how far the land is restored, from none to a clearing;
- which land lies beneath: any of the other six, cursed.

## 8. Depth in ink painting

- **The three distances.** High distance looks up from the foot of the mountain to its peak. Deep distance looks through the front of the mountain to the back. Level distance looks from near mountains to far ones [src](https://mimesisjournals.com/ojs/index.php/aisthesis/article/download/4221/3320) [src](https://projects.mcah.columbia.edu/jaanus/record/sanen). Their moods are abrupt, layered, and "blended and ethereal" [src](https://mimesisjournals.com/ojs/index.php/aisthesis/article/download/4221/3320). Guo Xi also moved the viewpoint within one picture (the "angle of totality": "a painting is not a window") [src](https://depts.washington.edu/chinaciv/painting/tptgnsla.htm).
- **Mist between the layers.** Across the river in Old Trees, Level Distance, the mountains are blurred to suggest mist, and the woodcutters fade into the distance [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Trees,_Level_Distance). "Hiding some details actually enables the painting to have depth" [src](https://oss.adm.ntu.edu.sg/liml0074/tag/chinese-painting/).
- **Value and colour by distance.** "The gradual desaturation of colour … conveys distance" [src](https://tangpoetry.substack.com/p/wang-wei-views-with-a-painterly-eye). The five grades of ink give tone its steps [src](https://www.inkston.com/community/forums/topic/five-grades-ink). Shōno uses three greys for three distances [src](https://www.clevelandart.org/art/126086).
- **Spacing and overlap.** Nearer land is drawn larger, so the gaps between parallel lines grow from the top of the picture to the bottom, without vanishing points [src](https://noteaccess.com/Texts/Wong/WongT2.htm). A shape repeated at two sizes ties near to far [src](https://madegooddesigns.com/?p=106559).
- **Scale cues.** Use the bean-sized person [src](https://berneval.hcommons.org/?p=5321), Fan Kuan's tiny mule train [src](https://depts.washington.edu/chinaciv/painting/tptgnsla.htm), and Sesshū's climber made of a few short, quick strokes, with a moored boat below [src](https://smarthistory.org/?p=43175). "Distant figures have no eyes" (attributed to Wang Wei), and "a vertical stroke of three inches represents a height of a thousand ren" (Zong Bing) [src](https://mimesisjournals.com/ojs/index.php/aisthesis/article/download/4221/3320).
- **A generator's spacing.** {Shan, Shui}* tries a mountain every 30 px, keeping it only at a local maximum of Perlin noise, and adds a grey far mountain every 1000 px. Each mountain is nested noisy arcs: the outermost one is filled and outlined, and the inner ones become texture [src](https://zverok.space/advent2021/day24).

## 9. What the games and renderers did

- The brush-painted wolf game is "sumi-e-inspired cel-shaded", in a "woodcut, watercolor style". Its PS2 version laid a "paper parchment filter" over everything [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Ckami).
- Its director found "representing nature in a Japanese style … very refreshing", and the ink-brush look led to wind drawn as trajectories [src](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wiiu/thewonderful101/0/4). Its visuals rarely carry "useless detail", and "ink strokes swirl through the air to signify flowing winds" [src](https://unwinnable.com/2012/06/18/okami/).
- A study of its look draws an edge where 0 ≤ N·V ≤ ε (ε tried from 0.2 to 1.0). It shades in ten grey levels with thresholds from 0.075 to 0.6, and lays paper in with an alpha of 1 minus the mean RGB [src](https://publications.scss.tcd.ie/theses/diss/2020/TCD-SCSS-DISSERTATION-2020-056.pdf).
- An image-based ink style sets each stroke's thickness by its curvature. It spreads ink by blurring and then sharpening, and adds paper-fibre masks [src](https://history.siggraph.org/?p=149263).
- Rock-texture synthesis needs only a contour and parameters: hemp-fiber for gentle slopes, axe-cut for hard rock [src](https://diglib.eg.org/items/90676dee-00d4-4b71-9b50-2e417cbd49bf/full).

## What we take

In priority order, each made as grey maps (silhouettes, heights, masks) for the existing chain to wash, light in toon bands and ink [est]:

1. **A Land choice.** The seed picks one of seven lands (a shared seed choice, so the parts agree). A new knob, Land, leans it, as Windward Sea's weather is leaned, so that at its middle any land can come. The six hues are full, so each land reuses them by role: Mountain Hue is the land's colour, Sun Hue the torii and the miasma, Blossom Hue the petals or the paddies' green.
2. **Depth bands.** Set three to seven bands (a Depth knob), each band one step of the five ink grades: charred near, clear far. Spacing grows toward the bottom. A mist mask lies at the foot of every band, and the colour dilutes toward the Paper Hue with distance.
3. **A scale cue per land.** One tiny thing, about 1/100 of the nearest peak's height: a boat, a climber of three strokes, a roof, a torii. It always sits on a mist gap, where it reads.
4. **Texture strokes as height.** Strokes are dashes thrown over each band's height map and turned by its slope. Axe-cut means short triangles across the contour on crags; hemp-fiber means long sinuous lines down domes, denser at their outer edges; Mi dots are horizontal wet blots. The Light Engine then lights the strokes too.
5. **Terraces for free.** Posterize the land's height into 8 to 20 steps and ink each step's edge (a bent-ribbon line). Each tread is a mirror of the sky's gradation, flipped and dimmed: paddies, and the moon in each.
6. **Snow by subtraction.** Snow inverts the ink. The lit band of the toon light becomes bare paper, ink stays only on the shadow side and under branches, and the sky darkens down to the hills. Flakes are holes cut from the sky and a fine gofun spatter.
7. **The curling wave.** The wave is a profile curve with a fold near its crest (Twirl). The crest edge is broken into rounded claws, and each claw is broken again at half size, for two self-similar levels. The hollow carries dark stripes, then a bright face; the foam is the Paper Hue barely tinted. The far sea is seigaiha arcs, and one small wave repeats a peak's shape.
8. **Bamboo.** Stalks are vertical bands broken at nodes on a short-long-short rhythm. The leaves are a press-and-lift picture thrown by Scatter in clumps of two and three, all leaning one way in wind. Three grey layers with mist between them make the grove, indigo in the farthest.
9. **Roofs and gates.** These are silhouettes standing on a band's ridge, grey for tile (a fine stripes map gives the rows) or thick and round-ridged for thatch. Add one torii (two posts and a lintel curved up, vermilion and black) and, now and then, a pagoda of three or five roofs whose eave tips lie on a straight line. A cloud band crosses the village.
10. **The great tree.** One trunk in the near band, with a crown of blossom clouds or dark evergreen. A rope band of diagonal twists wraps it, hung with zigzag shide. Fields lie at its feet (5).
11. **The miasma.** The bokashi inverted (red from the ground up), haboku blots, the wash desaturated, bare crab-claw branches, ash for petals. A Restore knob opens a clearing where colour, green and petals return.

## What this app can do that others don't [est]

- **The poem restores the land.** The miasma thins where the words sit, so the clearing is the poem's shape: the text is the brush.
- **The light dial moves the sun through the distances.** The toon bands turn on every ridge, and a low sun reddens only the near bands while the far ones stay clear and pale, as Guo Xi's colours ask.

# Prior art: life and views on the Ringworld (townsfolk, paths, clouds, water, turning sprites, grounding)

Key: **[src](URL)** = read in that source for this doc. **[est]** = my own estimate, inference or arithmetic. Researched 2026-10-22 by searching and opening each source. Companion to ringworld-time.md (the clocks, the sun, the windows' lit fraction), which this doc builds on rather than repeats. What could not be opened or confirmed is under **Gaps** at the end.

Ruby's notes on the last films: "Humans don't move? And should mostly try to go inside at night. Some objects still move in circles but it's better. Clouds should fade instead of jumping around. Minor water / ocean animations. Definitely up to 16 points of rotation for buildings. Make sure buildings are never floating in the air or on water. Overly wide objects rotate with the camera so either need more in-between stages or to be broken up into smaller objects and positioned individually (hedgerows etc) and sorted by the camera." Every effect stays photosensitivity-safe (AGENTS.md §11): no large region brightens or darkens suddenly.

## Summary

- **Townsfolk keep timetables, not physics.** Stardew Valley, Skyrim and Animal Crossing all give each person a list of (time, place, pose) and walk them between places [src](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Schedule_data) [src](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Bethesda_Tutorial_Packages). Real streets hold about 5% of the day's walkers between 23:00 and 06:00 [src](https://bikepeddocumentation.org/application/files/6114/6671/7593/NBPD_Adjustment_Factors.pdf), and darkness alone cuts walking by 38% [src](https://theconversation.com/when-commuters-vanish-the-effect-of-darkness-on-active-travel-84938).
- **Circles come from periodic sums.** A sum of sines closes into a loop when its frequency ratios are rational and never repeats when they are irrational [src](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2013/02/09/pretty-squiggles/). Steering (Reynolds' wander, path following, arrival) and waypoint timetables give long purposeful paths instead [src](https://ics-websites.science.uu.nl/docs/vakken/mcrws/papers_new/Reynolds%20-%201999%20-%20Steering%20behaviors%20for%20autonomous%20characters.pdf).
- **Clouds pop because a time-lapse samples too sparsely.** A camera is a sampler in time and fast motion aliases [src](https://tessive.com/time-filter-technical-explanation); "sharp, frozen motion can look jumpy while motion blur will create a smooth look" [src](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/explora/node/103791). Games keep each cloud's identity and change its alpha only by small steps [src](https://github.com/magcius/noclip.website/blob/main/src/ZeldaWindWaker/d_kankyo_wether.ts).
- **Stylized water is a few quiet layers.** Wind Waker uses one tiling line texture displaced by compound sines [src](https://medium.com/@gordonnl/the-ocean-170fdfd659f1); Sea of Thieves blurs its foam with feedback so it disperses [src](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2018-Talks-Ang_The-Technical-Art-of-Sea-of-Thieves.pdf). Fresh swell runs at 7–10 s and pure swell at over 10 s [src](https://www.weather.gov/media/mlb/marine/dominant_wave_period.pdf).
- **Sixteen views is the floor for smooth turning; blending hides the steps.** Doom drew 8 views at 45°, and its ports draw 16 at 22.5° [src](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Sprite). A billboard system with 64 views crossfades between them for "perfectly smooth blending" [src](https://www.leadwerks.com/community/blogs/entry/1563-billboards-part-2), and with no crossfade a switch "will produce a visible pop" [src](https://docs.unity3d.com/450/Documentation/ScriptReference/Terrain-treeCrossFadeLength.html).
- **Long things are many things.** Painter's sorting fails on long or overlapping pieces unless they are cut [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painter%27s_algorithm). Isometric games cut objects "into smaller cubes with seams that match" [src](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/470599-isometric-depth-sorting/).
- **Grounding is sampled, not assumed.** Engines test the footprint's corners [src](https://discussions.unity.com/t/prevent-the-player-from-building-stuff-on-an-unequal-terrain/176407), keep placements within slope and height ranges [src](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/open-world-tools-property-reference-in-unreal-engine), and give water an infinite cost for building [src](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/egalin/Articles/2012-villages.pdf).

**Where the Ringworld is now** [est, read in stepsRing.js]: a person is a still prop "for scale". Movers (RG_MOVES: helpers, drones, walkers, shepherds, sheep…) ride two or three sines, and drones and shepherds ride a true circle. Clouds ride a capped weather clock (0.7 s of wind a frame), small motions a 2.5 s clock, and sways the gentle rgSwayTime clock.

## 1. Ambient townsfolk: timetables, walking, and going in at night

**How games schedule a day**
- **Stardew Valley:** a schedule is a slash-separated list of "time location x y facing [animation]" entries, such as "610 Town 47 87 0". The time is when the NPC sets off; a time prefixed with "a" means arrive by then, and the game works out the start. A "square" animation makes random movement within a rectangle round the point, the day ends with a sleep pose at home (for example "2000 SeedShop 1 9 3 abigail_sleep"), and rainy days have their own list [src](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Schedule_data).
- **Skyrim (Creation Kit):** "An actor always runs the first valid package in the package stack". A package is named start-by-duration (Eat 8x1 runs from 8:00 to 9:00, Sleep 1x8 from 1:00), and the fallback at the bottom is a sandbox "near editor location, radius 1500" around the home. One game hour is 3 real minutes [src](https://ck.uesp.net/wiki/Bethesda_Tutorial_Packages). 1500 units is about 21 m, at Bethesda's 128 units to six feet [src](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/skyrim-s-modular-approach-to-level-design) [est].
- **Oblivion's guards** work shifts "just as real guards would": half a day at a post or on a patrol of named stops, then relieved, then off to the barracks to eat and sleep [src](https://content1.uesp.net/wiki/Oblivion:Imperial_City_Guard).
- **The Sims:** objects "advertise their ability to satisfy specific needs", and a Sim "selects from the top options at random to prevent robotic predictability" [src](https://gameindustrylibrary.com/documents/gmtk-the-genius-ai-behind-the-sims).
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** staggers bedtimes by personality: Normal villagers sleep from 00:00 to 06:00 [src](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Normal), Lazy from 23:00 to 08:00 [src](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Lazy), Cranky from 03:30 to 09:00 [src](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Cranky).
- **Red Dead Redemption 2** (three Valentine NPCs followed for a day): one leaves for farm work at 8:00, drinks at the saloon in the evening and goes home around midnight; one builds houses by day and falls asleep at a saloon table around 1:00; one chops wood, feeds animals and ends the day playing harmonica by his tent's fire [src](https://mein-mmo.de/en/3-npcs-red-dead-redemption-2-einen-tag,286499).
- **Octopath Traveler II:** the day and night cycle changes how NPCs move about town [src](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/octopath-traveler-ii-builds-a-bigger-bolder-world-in-its-stunning-hd-2d-style).
- **Assassin's Creed Unity:** 10,000 crowd NPCs on screen within "the limit of 40 real AIs and 120 high resolution models". A pool swaps low-detail people for full ones unnoticed, and a simplified brain animates the rest [src](https://gdcvault.com/play/1022141/Massive-Crowd-on-Assassin-s). Systemic crowd events (fistfights, pickpockets) arise from it [src](https://gdconf.com/article/learn-about-assassin-s-creed-unity-s-dynamic-crowds-at-gdc-2015/).
- **Townscaper** has no people at all. A reviewer wished for "people wandering the streets, or even light traffic" [src](https://www.pcworld.com/article/393157/townscaper-impressions-build-picturesque-fishing-villages-with-no-direction-and-no-drama.html).

**Walking**
- **Speed:** normal walking runs from 1.43 m/s (men aged 40–49) to 0.94 m/s (women aged 80–99) [src](https://bacpar.csp.org.uk/node/338279). Across studies, singles walk at 1.0–1.4 m/s and triads at 0.7–1.2; dyads are 30% slower than singles, and elderly groups 40% slower than other adult groups [src](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.13293).
- **Groups** (the same review): on a Toulouse shopping street 54% of people walked in groups at low density and 71% at moderate density. Dyads walk abreast about 80 cm apart (centre to centre), triads in a V with the middle one at least 20 cm back, and fours and fives in a U; groups narrow into river-like files in a crush [src](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.13293).
- **Pauses:** a waypoint carries a delay in ms (an example row waits 10,000) and a walk or run flag [src](https://www.azerothcore.org/wiki/waypoint_data).

**People out by hour** (percent of the 06–22 count; NBPD's weekday hourly factors, April–September) [src](https://bikepeddocumentation.org/application/files/6114/6671/7593/NBPD_Adjustment_Factors.pdf)

- **Night:** 23:00–06:00 holds "about 5% of the average daily total" [src](https://bikepeddocumentation.org/application/files/6114/6671/7593/NBPD_Adjustment_Factors.pdf). That is about 0.7% an hour, roughly 8% of the noon rate [est]. Minneapolis assumes 75% of walking falls between 6:30 and 18:30 [src](https://www2.minneapolismn.gov/media/content-assets/www2-documents/government/Pedestrian-and-Bicyclist-Count-Methodology.pdf).
- **Commercial towns** peak at noon and through the afternoon, with no commuter peaks, and their nights are "very low" [src](https://www.uvm.edu/~transctr/publications/trb2009/PedestrianTrafficVolumes-Aultman-Hall-Jan09.pdf). A bar street does the opposite: Fargo's Broadway is busiest "around midnight, with a steep drop in volume by 3AM" [src](https://fmmetrocog.org/application/files/4216/7933/9675/2023_Fargo-Moorhead_BikePed_Count_Report_Final_20230223.pdf).
- **Darkness at the same clock hour** cut pedestrians by 38% and cyclists by 27% (the clock-change method) [src](https://theconversation.com/when-commuters-vanish-the-effect-of-darkness-on-active-travel-84938).
- **Who stays out:** Georgian London's watch patrolled in pairs "from around 9pm until sunrise", with a lantern, calling "the hour and the state of the weather" at each strike; St George's parish kept 38 watchmen in 1735 [src](https://www.regencyhistory.net/2016/08/georgian-watchmen-security-on-night.html). Add the late walkers going home from the saloon around midnight [src](https://mein-mmo.de/en/3-npcs-red-dead-redemption-2-einen-tag,286499).
- **Windows** follow the lit fraction of ringworld-time.md §6, which peaks at 88% at 21:00. Here a window is lit by its own occupant arriving rather than by chance [est].

## 2. Paths that don't look like circles

- **Reynolds' wander:** a new random force each frame is "twitchy". Instead the steering target lives on a sphere slightly ahead of the character; each frame it moves by a small random displacement and is projected back onto the sphere, so it "takes a 'random walk' from one direction to another". The sphere's radius sets the strength and the displacement sets the rate [src](https://ics-websites.science.uu.nl/docs/vakken/mcrws/papers_new/Reynolds%20-%201999%20-%20Steering%20behaviors%20for%20autonomous%20characters.pdf).
- **Path following** (same paper): the path is a spine plus a radius. The character predicts its future position and projects it onto the spine; within the radius no steering is needed, and outside it seeks the projected point. **Arrival** ramps the speed down inside a slowing distance. The vehicle truncates its force to max_force and its velocity to max_speed [src](https://ics-websites.science.uu.nl/docs/vakken/mcrws/papers_new/Reynolds%20-%201999%20-%20Steering%20behaviors%20for%20autonomous%20characters.pdf).
- **Noise heading:** a classroom ant reads 2D noise at its position (noiseScale 0.03), maps it to a heading of 0–2π and steps at a fixed length [src](https://www.cs.middlebury.edu/~candrews/classes/cs467-s20/practicals/practical04).
- **Noise steps drift:** adding noise(tx) and noise(ty) steps to a position (tx 0, ty 10000, += 0.05) makes the walker "always" head toward the upper left [src](https://forum.processing.org/one/topic/beginner-needs-a-bit-of-advice-related-to-perlin-noise-randomness). Perlin output is not uniform, with a measured bias of −0.02 to −0.08, so accumulated steps drift; steps of 0.005–0.03 are advised [src](https://forum.processing.org/two/discussion/24879/).
- **Shared noise fields** can steer a crowd: each agent samples separate heading and speed fields at its position, so "nearby agents share trends without collapsing into identical motion". The field either drifts or is resampled, with jitter added against lockstep [src](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.18947).
- **Lissajous and its kin:** sin x + 0.7 sin(φx), with φ the golden ratio, "would never exactly repeat" because the ratio is irrational [src](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2013/02/09/pretty-squiggles/). Rational ratios close into loops [est]. Never repeating is still not purposeful: the figure keeps returning to the same centre [est].
- **Animals:** a correlated random walk has "a tendency to continue in the same direction" (von Mises turning angles around 0). The composite form switches between intensive search near food (Brownian) and "fast and nearly straight movement" between patches, and each mode persists over time [src](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1406.4355).
- **For an offline renderer** each frame is drawn alone at its own t, so steering cannot integrate a state from frame to frame. Instead, precompute each agent's timetable from its seed (waypoints, departure and arrival times, pauses) and make position(t) an interpolation along smoothed segments [est].

## 3. Clouds over a time-lapse

- **The real thing:** photographers shoot slow clouds every 10–20 s and storms every 2–5 s, and play back at 24–30 fps [src](https://www.miops.com/blogs/news/cloud-timelapse-photography-techniques-for-dynamic-skies). DxO uses 3 s for fast clouds, 5 s for slow clouds or fog, and 30 s for fog over 3 h [src](https://reference-dxoone.dxo.com/en/enatmospheric-clouds-fog-storms/). Clouds "are constantly shifting in light, shape, and density" [src](https://www.miops.com/blogs/news/cloud-timelapse-photography-techniques-for-dynamic-skies).
- **Lifetimes:** shallow cumulus living under 45 min are bubble-like [src](https://arm.gov/research/highlights/1185). A storm cell lasts 20 min to 1.5 h [src](https://old.forestencyclopedia.net/p/p388), or 45–60 min, going through growth, mature and dissipation stages; water evaporates "at the sides of the cloud as dry, environmental air is entrained" [src](https://rams.atmos.colostate.edu/at540/fall03/fall03Pt8.pdf). So clouds thin from their edges [est].
- **Why it pops:** a movie camera samples in time, so motion above the Nyquist rate (12 Hz at 24 fps) is "aliased, or falsely shifted" [src](https://tessive.com/time-filter-technical-explanation). This is temporal aliasing, the wagon-wheel effect; the exposure time decides how strong the aliases are [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliasing). At 2 min of world time a frame, a 10 m/s cumulus moves 1.2 km a frame, more than its own width, and a 30 min cloud lives only 15 frames. Successive frames then share no recognizable cloud, and the sky reads as jumping [est].
- **The photographer's remedy** is a shutter of half the interval (a 1 min interval takes a 30 s exposure), using ND filters if needed, because "motion blur will create a smooth look" [src](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/explora/node/103791).
- **Wind Waker** (noclip's port of its weather code, as read for ringworld-time.md §4): each cloud is a persistent billboard. Its alpha eases toward a target by at most 0.1 a frame, it thins in at the dome's edge and out near the top, and it is moved to a new place only while invisible [src](https://github.com/magcius/noclip.website/blob/main/src/ZeldaWindWaker/d_kankyo_wether.ts).
- **Sea of Thieves:** cloud meshes are spread over a square several miles across. The wind offsets and wraps them round the player, and high-pressure zones open gaps that fade the clouds out and push them away; distant clouds take an alpha threshold for crisper, cartoon edges [src](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2018-Talks-Ang_The-Technical-Art-of-Sea-of-Thieves.pdf).
- **Horizon Zero Dawn:** coverage and cloud type are weather-map channels that "a simulation that progresses during gameplay" modulates. Rain moves the map toward cumulonimbus at 70% coverage, curl noise distorts the shapes, and the cirrus and alto layers are 2D textures "tiling and scrolling at different speeds and directions" [src](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2015/The%20Real-time%20Volumetric%20Cloudscapes%20of%20Horizon%20-%20Zero%20Dawn%20-%20ARTR.pdf).
- **Dissolve:** noise compared against an animated threshold lets a shape appear or vanish piece by piece rather than as one uniform fade [src](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/unity-shader-graph-quick-tutorial---dissolve-shader).

## 4. Water that moves a little

- **Wind Waker's sea:** one tiling texture is used as a mask for white lines, then again in dark blue with its lookup offset. Its UVs are displaced by "four sine curves... each with a different scale and offset", and the height is three sines (time ×1.0, 1.5 and 0.4; frequency 1.0, 2.3 and 3.3) [src](https://medium.com/@gordonnl/the-ocean-170fdfd659f1). Black and white masks drive its ocean, fire and particles [src](https://medium.com/@gordonnl/wind-waker-graphics-analysis-a0b575a31127).
- **Sea of Thieves:** foam forms at wave peaks and round anything crossing the surface. The foam buffer "is progressively blurred with feedback" so it disperses, then blends with painted textures; calm water foams only round objects [src](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2018-Talks-Ang_The-Technical-Art-of-Sea-of-Thieves.pdf).
- **Nimue** (citing Wind Waker for shore foam lines, RiME for wind waves and Sea of Thieves for foam patterns): edge foam stays at the intersection while edge waves move outward from it; two panned normal samples, one faster and stronger, show the wind's direction. It used "very reduced gravity waves", kept the specular very low, masked out distant waves and chose a medium water tone so the water would not pull focus [src](https://80.lv/articles/how-to-build-stylized-water-shader-design-implementation-for-nimue/).
- **Toon water for the web:** the foam line is the depth difference under 0.7, the water lines are dimmed to 0.1 and scroll at 0.02 texture units a second, and the foam takes "a color similar to that of the static water lines" [src](https://code.tutsplus.com/creating-toon-water-for-the-web-part-2--cms-30485t). RiME's water VFX are "cascade, ripples" [src](https://www.unrealengine.com/events/unreal-fest-europe-2018/stylized-vfx-in-rime), and The Last of Us Part II has "Edge Ripples" and a beach "curl wave" [src](https://gdcvault.com/play/1027072/Creative-and-Experimental-VFX-in).
- **Periods:** local wind waves run 3–6 s, fresh swell 7–10 s and pure swell over 10 s; one buoy logged swell at 12.1–12.9 s and wind waves at 3.6–4.0 s [src](https://www.weather.gov/media/mlb/marine/dominant_wave_period.pdf). In deep water L = 1.56 T² m [src](https://geo.libretexts.org/Courses/American_Meteorological_Society/Introduction_to_Ocean_Sciences_(Segar)/09%3A_Waves/9.07%3A_Deep-Water_Waves), so C = 1.56 T m/s: a 10 s swell is 156 m long and runs at 15.6 m/s [est].
- **Ripples:** capillary waves are at most 1.73 cm long, with a minimum speed of 23.1 cm/s [src](https://britannica.com/print/article/93834). A ring from a dropped insect therefore spreads at about 0.2–0.3 m/s [est].
- **Swash:** each broken wave's uprush is faster and shorter than its backwash, the backwash runs perpendicular to the shore, and the motion's time-scale "varies from seconds to minutes" [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swash).
- **Under a time-lapse** [est]: swell, swash and glints are periodic at seconds, so at minutes a frame their phase is random every frame, which is shimmer, not motion. Real long exposures smooth such water to a sheen (the B&H rule above). Water motion therefore belongs on the film clock (ringworld-time.md §10's τ), not on t.

## 5. Sprite rotation views, blending, and long objects

- **Doom:** 8 rotations at 45° steps, where "TROOA2A8" draws rotation 2 and, mirrored, rotation 8. Rotation 0 is one graphic for every angle (weapons, corpses, simple decorations). EDGE, PrBoom+ and ZDoom extend this "to 16 angles, in 22.5-degree increments" [src](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Sprite).
- **How many:** "16 directions minimum, if not 32", more for larger sprites because they change more per degree. StarCraft drew 9 and mirrored 7 to make 16. Powers of two keep a view on each cardinal direction, and mirroring swaps an asymmetric object's sides [src](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/594196-2d-sprites-how-many-directions-for-smooth-rotation/).
- **Factorio:** the car has "64 separated directions" [src](https://forums.factorio.com/viewtopic.php?p=93117). RotatedSprite takes any direction_count (the radar uses 64), and its mirror option "is not usable once the picture contains shadows" [src](https://testing-wiki.factorio.com/Types/RotatedSprite).
- **Blending:** Leadwerks renders "64 different view angles... in a circle" and blends between them by "cross-fading and cross-dissolving", with a top quad dissolved in [src](https://www.leadwerks.com/community/blogs/entry/1563-billboards-part-2). Unity's tree crossfade at 0 "will produce a visible pop" [src](https://docs.unity3d.com/450/Documentation/ScriptReference/Terrain-treeCrossFadeLength.html).
- **Octahedral impostors** grew from Ryan Brucks' Fortnite work [src](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/octahedral-impostors-for-three-js/80318). One port recommends a 16 by 16 grid, on a hemisphere for foliage seen from the side [src](https://github.com/SIsilicon/Godot-Octahedral-Impostors). A browser build traced its pop to quantizing the view "into one of 25 cells". Blending the four nearest tiles makes neighbouring views cross-fade instead of snap, and the build moved to 24 ring views 15° apart [src](https://app.cinevva.com/blog/2026-05-04-open-world-browser-part-19-imposters).
- **Octopath Traveler II** (flat sprites in 3D) turned its camera "nearly 90 degrees" in battles and 180 in events, and asked "how far we could rotate the camera without compromising graphical fidelity" [src](https://www.unrealengine.com/developer-interviews/octopath-traveler-ii-builds-a-bigger-bolder-world-in-its-stunning-hd-2d-style).
- **The step you see** [est]: between neighbouring views a corner r px from the sprite's axis jumps 2r sin(Δ/2). That is 0.39r at 16 views, 0.20r at 32 and 0.10r at 64, so a 120 px building (r 60) jumps 23 px at 16 views. A jump of a few pixels passes unnoticed; tens of pixels need a crossfade.
- **Painter's sorting:** it paints far to near, but cyclic overlaps and piercing polygons "must be cut to allow sorting"; Newell's algorithm and BSP trees do the cutting [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painter%27s_algorithm). With one depth per sprite, large overlapping objects fail; the fixes are to cut them "into smaller cubes with seams that match" or to restrict the editor, and trees split into trunk and canopy [src](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/470599-isometric-depth-sorting/). Cycles occur even among sprites that don't intersect, leaving "an imperfect sort" or a split [src](https://hero.handmade.network/forums/code-discussion/t/1331/p/7515). Casey Muratori: "it is only relevant which is closer where they overlap". Multi-tile objects can be built from several entities, each sorted by its footprint on the ground [src](https://hero.handmade.network/forums/game-discussion/t/1293/p/7274).
- **Modular kits:** pieces "snap together using a grid system". Footprints are multiples of each other (512 tiles with 256), variants carry 01/02/03 suffixes, and repeated detail causes "art fatigue" sooner than repeated architecture [src](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/skyrim-s-modular-approach-to-level-design).
- **Why wide things seem to turn** [est]: a sprite is one picture pinned at one point and one depth, so a hedgerow drawn as one sprite swings round its middle when the camera turns, and sorts wrongly against everything along its length.

## 6. Grounding: never floating, never on water

- **Unreal foliage:** Z Offset (a min to max range), Align to Normal, Ground Slope Angle ("only... surfaces sloping in the specified angle range"), Height (a valid altitude range), Landscape Layers, Collision with World (an overlap test before each placement) and Random Pitch Angle [src](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/open-world-tools-property-reference-in-unreal-engine).
- **Unreal PCG:** Normal To Density against up (0, 0, 1), then a Density Filter keeping 0.85–1, so slopes of 32° or less [est, arccos 0.85]. A spline with ±3 m bounds is subtracted to clear "a roughly 6 m wide walkway" [src](https://uhiyama-lab.com/en/notes/ue/pcg-basics/).
- **Alignment:** "0 keeps objects upright, 1 aligns fully to the surface normal". Trees on slopes take 0.2–0.4, since full alignment makes trunks look like they are falling, rocks about 1, and every pivot goes to its bottom centre [src](https://superhivemarket.com/products/terrain-scatter-scatter-trees-and-rocks-on-any-terrain-with-density-painting/docs).
- **Sinking:** a pivot at the centre puts half a model underground [src](https://discussions.unity.com/t/gameobject-placing-halfway-through-ground/178853). Trees are sunk deliberately by moving the pivot "so the tree just moves more into the terrain" [src](https://www.leadwerks.com/community/topic/2695-planting-trees-in-terrain), and "taller and steeper terrains require a greater change" [src](https://www.moddb.com/tutorials/vegetation-tutorial). Players notice foundations that "'sink', or 'float above'" the ground by a pixel or two [src](https://forums.funcom.com/t/uneven-levels-of-ground-level-for-items-foundations/170554).
- **Footprints:** "Make four raycasts - one at each corner of the building", and allow placement only when the heights agree within a tolerance [src](https://discussions.unity.com/t/prevent-the-player-from-building-stuff-on-an-unequal-terrain/176407).
- **Villages** (Emilien et al.): each building type has a bell-shaped slope preference and a prohibited range. An impossible criterion scores −1 and vetoes the site; "a farm cannot be built in the middle of a lake", and water costs +∞. Houses are still drawn toward shores, face "the closest normal to the road", and move their doors and windows to avoid hitting the ground on steep slopes [src](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/egalin/Articles/2012-villages.pdf). Bethesda's kits found 30–45° slopes look better than the 60° their AI could climb [src](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/skyrim-s-modular-approach-to-level-design).
- **The exceptions that stand in water:** stilt houses are "elevated on stilts above the ground or water body", built against floods and vermin in wetlands, on lake shores and over the sea: Bajau, Tonlé Sap, Chiloé's palafitos [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stilt_house). Pier foundations raise a floor on posts with a skirt over uneven or flood-prone ground, for example 2 ft piers on a 10 ft grid [src](https://www.cadsoft.com/pier-foundations/).

## 7. What we take (for stepsRing.js, paintKit.js and the film's clocks) [est unless tagged]

**Townsfolk**
- Each person gets a seeded timetable of stops (home door → work → square or saloon → home), Stardew-style "time place pose" entries with "arrive by" times [src](https://stardewvalleywiki.com/Modding:Schedule_data). Between stops they walk; at a stop they stand, work or sit, with a small square of idle shuffle round the point.
- **Wake** 05:30–09:00 and **bed** 21:00–01:00 (about 1 in 10 a night owl until 03:30), after Animal Crossing's spread [src](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Cranky). Leave for work at 07:30–08:30, gather in the square at 12–13, home at 17–19, and send about 30% back out from 19:00 to 22:00.
- **People outside** follow §1's Town or Country row scaled to its peak, then about 0.08 of peak from 23:00 to 05:00 (so 1–3 people in a 30-person village). They are the watch (a pair with one lantern, 21:00 to dawn, stopping each hour), a late walker or two between 23:00 and 01:00, and a lamplighter at dusk [src](https://www.regencyhistory.net/2016/08/georgian-watchmen-security-on-night.html). Where night falls before 21:00 on the ring, scale evening walkers by 0.62 [src](https://theconversation.com/when-commuters-vanish-the-effect-of-darkness-on-active-travel-84938). Neon worlds flip this and peak near midnight [src](https://fmmetrocog.org/application/files/4216/7933/9675/2023_Fargo-Moorhead_BikePed_Count_Report_Final_20230223.pdf).
- **Speeds** run from 1.25–1.4 m/s for adults down to 0.95 for the old; pairs walk 30% slower, abreast 0.8 m apart; threes walk in a V. About 50% of townsfolk walk in groups [src](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2107.13293), and 15% of country folk [est]. Pause 2–15 s at turns and gates, and 1–10 min to chat in the square.
- **Doors:** people appear and vanish only at doors, fading over at least 6 frames. A home's windows light 10–60 s of t after the first person arrives and go dark at the last one's bedtime, each window easing over at least 6 frames, never blinking.
- **Under a time-lapse:** the timetable runs on t; strides and idle shuffles run on a walk clock capped at 0.4 s a frame (a 0.5 m step). A trip that would need more frames than the time left before the next stop becomes a fade out at one door and in at the other.

**Paths**
- Retire the circles (drone and shepherd) and the two- or three-sine wanders. Each mover gets a timetable of waypoints, chosen by a seeded walk on a graph (roads, field edges, the shore, inspection points). It runs along Catmull-Rom-smoothed segments at its speed, slows over the last 1–2 body lengths [src](https://ics-websites.science.uu.nl/docs/vakken/mcrws/papers_new/Reynolds%20-%201999%20-%20Steering%20behaviors%20for%20autonomous%20characters.pdf) and pauses there, so position(t) needs no stored state.
- **Animals:** graze mode drifts 0.05–0.2 m/s with wide turning (von Mises κ about 1) for 1–10 min; travel mode walks 0.8–1.2 m/s nearly straight (κ about 8) to a new patch. The modes persist, as in the composite walk [src](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/1406.4355).
- **Drones** fly straight legs at 3–8 m/s between inspection points and hover 3–20 s with a 0.1–0.3 m bob on the sway clock. When one orbits a point, its radius wanders ±30% and it leaves after under one turn. **Robots** follow roads with Reynolds' path radius set to half the lane.
- **Free wander** (birds, insects, idle robots): steer by noise heading (turn rate from 1D noise of t/60–300 s) at a constant speed, never by noise steps, with a soft leash back to home beyond its radius [src](https://forum.processing.org/two/discussion/24879/). If sines remain anywhere, use three or more at incommensurate ratios (1, 1.618, 2.414) with no term dominant [src](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2013/02/09/pretty-squiggles/).

**Clouds**
- Each cloud is a seeded entity with a birth time, a lifetime of 20–60 min of t, and a position of birth point + wind × age. Nothing is re-seeded per frame and no cloud ever jumps. It grows, holds and thins away, as a cumulus cell does [src](https://rams.atmos.colostate.edu/at540/fall03/fall03Pt8.pdf): opacity rises over the first 25% of its life and falls over the last 35%. It thins as a dissolve against noise, edges first [src](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/unity-shader-graph-quick-tutorial---dissolve-shader); its shape breathes but keeps its identity; it is respawned only while invisible [src](https://github.com/magcius/noclip.website/blob/main/src/ZeldaWindWaker/d_kankyo_wether.ts).
- **Film-frame limits:** a cloud's alpha may change at most 0.04 a frame (so a whole fade spans at least 25 frames, 0.8 s at 30 fps), and its centre may move at most 15% of its own width a frame. When t outruns these, stretch the cloud's life and slow its art wind rather than let it jump, as the weather clock already caps drift.
- **Smoothing:** if a cloud still moves more than 5% of its width a frame, draw it as 3 copies spread over half the frame's interval at a third of the alpha each (B&H's half-interval shutter [src](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/explora/node/103791)). Cirrus are long strips scrolling at their own speed, and cloud shadows inherit the same alpha limits, so no part of the land darkens by more than 4% a frame.

**Water**
- **Swell:** 2–3 sines of 7–12 s period plus chop of 3–6 s [src](https://www.weather.gov/media/mlb/marine/dominant_wave_period.pdf), drawn as Wind Waker-style line masks drifting along the wind [src](https://medium.com/@gordonnl/the-ocean-170fdfd659f1). They move 1–2 px at export near the shore and are masked out beyond about 1 km, as Nimue masks its distant waves [src](https://80.lv/articles/how-to-build-stylized-water-shader-design-implementation-for-nimue/).
- **Shore foam:** a band at the coastline that advances 0.5–2 m in 40% of the swell period and retreats in 60%, fading as it retreats (uprush is faster than backwash [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swash)). A second, fainter line follows half a period behind, and the foam's colour stays near the water lines' [src](https://code.tutsplus.com/creating-toon-water-for-the-web-part-2--cms-30485t).
- **Glints** are specks 1–3 px at export in the sun's path. Each lives 0.6–2 s, rising and falling smoothly, drifting at 0.3–1 m/s down the wind; together they cover under 0.5% of the frame.
- **Ripple rings** come from fish, insects or rain on still water: they spread at 0.25 m/s, just above the 23.1 cm/s capillary minimum [src](https://britannica.com/print/article/93834), for 1.5 s, fading to nothing, with at most 3 in view per 100 m².
- **Clocks:** all water motion runs on the film clock (τ, at most 0.25–0.5 s a frame), whatever t does; only the tide level and wet sand follow t.

**Buildings and wide things**
- **Views:** a building has 16 views, 22.5° apart [src](https://doomwiki.org/wiki/Sprite), chosen by its own bearing to the camera.
- **Crossfade** between neighbouring views, as Leadwerks does [src](https://www.leadwerks.com/community/blogs/entry/1563-billboards-part-2): hold each view through the middle 40% of its step and blend linearly across the rest.
- **Mirroring:** each view's maps are lit by the scene's light after any mirroring, since a mirror fails once shadows are baked in [src](https://testing-wiki.factorio.com/Types/RotatedSprite). A symmetric building then needs only 9 drawn views, as StarCraft's 9 + 7 [src](https://gamedev.net/forums/topic/594196-2d-sprites-how-many-directions-for-smooth-rotation/).
- **Large buildings:** above 150 px at export, draw the walls as projected quads from the building's true corners instead.
- **Splitting:** anything whose footprint is longer than 1.5 times its height, or longer than 6 m, is not one sprite. Hedgerows become bushes every 1–2 m overlapping 25%. Fences become posts at 2–3 m with rails drawn as projected 3D lines between post tops. Long buildings become bays of 4–6 m (variants 01/02/03 [src](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/skyrim-s-modular-approach-to-level-design)) plus gable ends. Walls become projected strips cut at every 4 m.
- **Sorting:** each piece sorts by the camera depth of its own footprint's nearest point, then far to near [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Painter%27s_algorithm). Any remaining cycle is cut at its overlap [src](https://hero.handmade.network/forums/code-discussion/t/1331/p/7515).

**Grounding**
- **Sample** the ground at the footprint's four corners and centre [src](https://discussions.unity.com/t/prevent-the-player-from-building-stuff-on-an-unequal-terrain/176407), giving slope = (max − min) / width.
- **Slope bands** (a veto by slope, as in the villages paper [src](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/egalin/Articles/2012-villages.pdf); the numbers are mine): up to 6° stands directly on the ground; 6–20° stands on a plinth, stone foundation or stilts on the downhill side; over 20° is no site for a building (terraced or cliff worlds excepted).
- **Heights:** floor = max corner + 0.1 m. The foundation runs from the floor down to the min corner and 0.3 m beyond, so it vanishes into the soil and no corner floats.
- **Small things** sink by 3% of their height (at least 0.1 m), plus width × tan(slope) / 2 on slopes, sinking deeper where the ground is steeper [src](https://www.moddb.com/tutorials/vegetation-tutorial). Trees stay upright (alignment 0.2–0.4), rocks follow the normal (about 1) [src](https://superhivemarket.com/products/terrain-scatter-scatter-trees-and-rocks-on-any-terrain-with-density-painting/docs), and buildings never tilt.
- **Water:** no building in a lake [src](https://perso.liris.cnrs.fr/egalin/Articles/2012-villages.pdf). Reject a site if any sample lies within 0.3 m of the water level, and keep a 2 m setback from the shoreline [est].
- **Only stilt houses, boathouses, piers and boats** may stand in water [src](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stilt_house): stilts reach the bed, and the floor sits at least 1 m above the water [est].
- **Test:** after placement, assert that every foot corner's projected ground is within 1 px at export of the drawn foot, and fail the review sheet otherwise.

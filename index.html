Page Text (Poetry Markup Language)

PML Help

Inline styles

Freely mixable and nestable: **bold**, *italic*, _underline_, ~~strike~~

Accents & gradients

[one] {two}
accent one, accent two
{[text]} [{text]}
blend one accent into the other
[text/lg] [text/rg]
fade an accent into the normal ink
{text/lg} {text/rg}
same, using accent two

Emoji inside an accent or gradient span tint to match.

Line types

##
large heading
-#
small aside
>
quote — left bar, tilt, slight transparency
#D
drop cap — first letter enlarged
#S
small caps — lowercase becomes small capitals

Rhyme markers

End a line with ~A ~B ~C ~D to tag its rhyme group — shown as a small colored bar, not as text. A and B use the accents; C and D use their split-complements. Combines with justification: text~A/r

Justification

/l /c /r — or /left /center /right — at the end of a line.

Split one line between alignments using segments:
left text <right text/r>
<left/l><center/c><right/r>

Segments <…>

Isolate part of a line. Segments accept any inline style above, plus:

/#:ff00ff
custom color
/f:4
font, by index (below)
/scale:150
size, percent of the fitted size
/track:150
letter-spacing, percent
/basis:50
raise; negative lowers
/jitter:150
per-letter hand jitter, percent
/grad:1#f00,2#00f
custom gradient, up to 4 stops
/rainbow /trans /lesbian
preset gradients; add :rev to reverse
/fx1,#abc,3
outline — color, thickness
/fx2,#f0f,10,4,4
shadow — color, blur, x, y
/fx0
drop effects for this segment only

Every directive has a sane bare form: /scale, /track, /jitter, /basis, /fx1, /fx2.

Chain as many as you like:
<text/#:ff00ff/f:4/scale:150/r/fx2,#fff,10,4,4>

Escaping

Prefix any special character with \ to print it literally: \[text\] prints [text]

# PoetryPress

A press for poems, in a browser. (Inside, in its code, it is Unfixable Vellum.)

Write the words. Choose a typeface, a ground, a surface for them to lie on.
Save the page as an image, with its words inside it.

**[poetrypress.unfixable.place](https://poetrypress.unfixable.place)**

Made on a phone, without a computer, by one person. Free, and it stays free.

---

### What is there

- A small markup for poems, with accents, asides and drop caps
  ([PML.md](PML.md), or the Arcane Guidance in the app).
- Close to fifty surfaces, from film grain to a card game left on a table,
  each with four readings: 🜂 🜄 🜁 🜃.
- 🜉 The Athanor, where new surfaces are made from the parts of the old.
- Spells: whole looks, kept and shared by name. The Grimoire, for the poems
  themselves, dated by the moon.
- Every seed is a phrase.
- Locks, for what should not change.
- Images that carry their own alt text — the poem, the credit, a
  description — and offer it for pasting where you post them.
- It works offline once visited, and installs as an app.

Some of it is found by holding things down. Some by pressing `?`.

### The four elements

| | Element | Palette | Feeling |
|---|---|---|---|
| ♡ | **Whimsy** | red, purple, blue | sleep |
| √ | **Sharpness** | off-white, blue-black, metal | waking |
| ∆ | **Chaos** | sigils, noise, broken geometry | pain |
| 🜚 | **Touch** | neutrals only | contact |
